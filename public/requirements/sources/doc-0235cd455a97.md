This is an initial extensible catalogue, not a complete medical classification. Search aliases are discovery terms, not a statement that all matched labels are clinically equivalent. Related categories remain separate entries; selecting a search result never adds any other condition.

## Design and Meaning

- These are labelled KinForge pictograms, not standardized diagnostic symbols. Small modifiers must not be used without their text label or an accessible equivalent.
- Every person can have multiple separately recorded disability entries. Adding cerebral palsy does not add paralysis, an intellectual disability, wheelchair use, or any access requirement.
- Disability records and access preferences have separate picker categories and profile groups. Neither is derived from the other.
- The user can preserve preferred identity wording, such as Deaf, or qualify an uncertain historical record, such as Possible CP - unconfirmed, using the editable Record label.
- Details and optional source links carry specifics such as affected areas, side, historical wording or uncertainty. The icon does not encode an unrecorded severity, cause, permanence or ability.
- The previous general disability entry and all existing access-needs IDs remain valid. Unrecognized imported glyph IDs keep their recorded text under Other annotations.
- Vector art uses licensed Lucide pictograms and small compositions of those pictograms, with the same rounded-line treatment as the rest of the KinForge set. The app and SVG export share one artwork renderer.

## Privacy and Verification

All new entries are private. Existing public-facing export paths omit them; full backups retain them. The local workspace and backups are not encrypted by this feature. No personal medical information was uploaded or published.

31 automated tests passed, including 7 added for this clarification. The local production build passed. Browser checks covered category separation, CP and amputee search, a custom-label save/reload/edit cycle, an independent captions entry, mobile layout and visible Save/Cancel controls. Test-only records were cleared.

The live website, native installers and chart-level disability badges were not updated or released in this pass. No clinical validation or comprehensive assistive-technology audit is claimed.

## Terminology References

The CP expansion was checked against [NINDS: Cerebral Palsy](https://www.ninds.nih.gov/health-information/disorders/cerebral-palsy). The decision to keep individual needs separate from broad disability labels is consistent with the diversity described in [WHO: Disability](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/disability-and-health). These sources do not endorse or supply the KinForge glyph designs.
