## MyHeritage features

### Family-tree building

- Create and maintain an online family tree.
- Add relatives, relationships, dates, places, biographies, photographs, documents and sources.
- Use different tree views, including family, pedigree, fan and list views.
- Colour-code different ancestral branches.
- Import and export family trees using GEDCOM files.
- Invite relatives to view or contribute to a shared family site.
- Create charts, reports and family books.
- Use the downloadable **Family Tree Builder** desktop software.
- Access and update trees through the MyHeritage mobile application.

### Family-tree research tools

- **Smart Matches™** – compares people in your tree with people in other members’ family trees.
- **Record Matches** – connects people in your tree with potentially relevant historical records.
- **Instant Discoveries™** – adds groups of relatives or information from a confirmed discovery.
- **Theory of Family Relativity™** – combines family trees, historical records and DNA matches to suggest possible relationships.
- **Tree Consistency Checker** – identifies possible mistakes such as unlikely dates, incorrect relationships and duplicated people.
- **PedigreeMap™** – plots important family events and locations on a map.
- Family timelines and relationship reports.
- Notifications when new matches or records are found.
- Search variations that can account for alternative names and spellings.

### Historical records

MyHeritage advertises access to approximately **40 billion historical records**. [myheritage.com](https://www.myheritage.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Collections can include:

- Birth, baptism, marriage, divorce and death records.
- Census and population records.
- Immigration and passenger records.
- Military records.
- Newspapers and obituaries.
- Church and religious records.
- Burial and cemetery records.
- School and yearbook records.
- Electoral registers and directories.
- Government, court and legal records.
- Family trees contributed by other members.
- Family-history books and genealogical publications.

### MyHeritage DNA

- Autosomal DNA testing using a cheek-swab collection kit.
- **Ethnicity Estimate** showing ancestral ethnic regions.
- **Genetic Groups** identifying more specific populations and migration patterns.
- **DNA Matches** with potential biological relatives.
- Estimated relationship ranges for matches.
- Shared-DNA amounts shown in centimorgans.
- Shared ancestral surnames and locations.
- **Chromosome Browser** for examining shared DNA segments.
- **AutoClusters** grouping DNA matches who may share a common ancestor.
- **Theory of Family Relativity™** relationship suggestions.
- Filtering, sorting, labelling and messaging DNA matches.
- Map-based display of ancestral family events.
- DNA results can include ethnicity information, Genetic Groups, migration patterns, common names and matching relatives. [MyHeritage](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/12852544-what-is-the-myheritage-ethnicity-estimate?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Photograph and media tools

This is one of MyHeritage’s most distinctive areas.

- **MyHeritage In Color™** – colourises black-and-white photographs and restores faded colours.
- **Photo Enhancer** – sharpens blurred faces and improves resolution.
- **Photo Repair** – repairs scratches, creases and other damage.
- **Deep Nostalgia™** – animates an individual face in a still photograph.
- **LiveMemory™** – uses AI to turn an entire still photograph into a short animated video.
- **Photo Scanner** – scans photographs and documents using a mobile device.
- **Reimagine** – scans, restores, organises and shares family photographs.
- **Photo Storyteller™** – records a spoken story connected to a photograph.
- Photograph tagging and automatic face detection.
- Online albums for organising family photographs and videos.

MyHeritage’s current photo subscription includes tools such as Photo Enhancer, In Color, Deep Nostalgia and LiveMemory. [MyHeritage](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/12852561-what-is-the-photo-subscription?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Additional features

- Website and applications available in numerous languages.
- Mobile applications for iOS and Android.
- Private messaging between members and potential relatives.
- Family websites for sharing discoveries.
- Calendar reminders for family birthdays and anniversaries.
- Legacy Family Tree webinars and genealogy education.
- Privacy controls for trees, photographs, profiles and DNA results.

## Ancestry features

### Family-tree building

- Create one or more online family trees.
- Add relatives, relationships, dates, places, photographs, stories, documents and sources.
- Create public or private trees.
- Invite relatives or researchers to view or edit a tree.
- Import and export GEDCOM files.
- Link historical records directly to people in a tree.
- Attach DNA results to a family tree.
- View trees using pedigree, family and other visual layouts.
- Access and edit family trees through the Ancestry mobile application.

### Family-tree research tools

- **Ancestry Hints®** – suggests records, photographs and other family trees that may relate to someone in your tree.
- Suggested parents, relatives and other potential connections.
- Search historical records directly from a person’s profile.
- Source citations and record attachments.
- LifeStory-style chronological summaries of a person’s life.
- Maps showing where important life events occurred.
- Member-to-member messaging.
- Collaborative tree editing and sharing.
- AI-assisted transcription of uploaded handwritten documents in supported languages. [ancestry.com](https://www.ancestry.com/c/ancestry-blog/ancestry-news/document-transcription-feature?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Historical records

Ancestry provides access to billions of searchable family-history records, including:

- Birth, baptism, marriage and death records.
- Census and voter records.
- Immigration, naturalisation and passenger records.
- Military records.
- Newspapers, obituaries and funeral notices.
- Church and religious records.
- Wills, probate and court records.
- School and yearbook collections.
- Employment and occupational records.
- City and telephone directories.
- Land and property records.
- Family-history books.
- Public family trees created by members.

Access to particular international collections depends on the selected membership.

### AncestryDNA

- Autosomal DNA testing using a saliva sample.
- Ancestral-region and origins estimates.
- Results covering thousands of geographic places and populations.
- **Ancestral Journeys** identifying more recent communities, migration patterns and settlement histories.
- **DNA Matches** with potential biological relatives.
- Relationship estimates based on shared DNA.
- **SideView™** – divides matches, ancestral origins, journeys and traits between the two parental sides.
- Common ancestor and family-tree connections.
- Shared matches.
- Common surnames found in linked family trees.
- **Chromosome Painter** showing where ancestral regions appear in the user’s DNA.
- **AncestryDNA Traits** providing information about certain inherited characteristics.
- Filtering, grouping, notes and messaging for DNA matches.

Ancestry states that its database contains more than **25 million DNA samples**, while SideView can separate results by parental side without requiring both parents to test. [AncestryDNA Learning Hub](https://www.ancestry.com/c/dna-learning-hub/how-does-ancestrydna-test-differ-from-others?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Ancestry Pro Tools

Ancestry offers **Pro Tools** as a paid add-on to an active family-history membership. Features include:

- **Tree Checker** – identifies possible errors or inconsistencies.
- **Tree Mapper** – displays ancestral events geographically.
- Advanced tree filters and reports.
- **Smart Filters** for locating people who meet particular criteria.
- **Enhanced Shared Matches** – shows how much DNA selected matches share with one another.
- **Matches by Cluster** – groups interconnected DNA matches.
- Additional tools for organising and reviewing large trees.
- Advanced DNA and family-tree analysis.

Pro Tools is not included automatically in standard memberships. [Ancestry](https://www.ancestry.com/c/allproducts?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Photograph, story and media features

- Upload photographs, documents, audio and stories.
- Attach media to particular people and life events.
- Create galleries for individuals in a tree.
- Tag people appearing in photographs.
- Scan and upload photographs through the mobile application.
- Add written family stories and memories.
- Use AI transcription for eligible handwritten documents.
- Share discoveries with invited family members.

Ancestry has useful media storage, but it does not currently offer the same extensive collection of photograph colourisation, repair and animation tools as MyHeritage.

### Additional features and services

- Mobile applications for iOS and Android.
- Ancestry Academy educational content.
- Research guides and virtual learning events.
- Member messaging and collaborative family trees.
- Privacy settings for trees and DNA results.
- Record collections from many countries.
- Integration with related services and collections, depending on the membership, including **Newspapers.com®** and **Fold3®**.
- Options to download raw AncestryDNA data.
- Regular updates to DNA regions, journeys and matching tools.
