# KinForge Report Catalogue Requirements

Requested 23 September 2026. This document supplements, rather than replaces, Ideas.md and previous generated requirements documents.

## User's Requested Reports

1. Person, Family and Kinship Reports: formal write-ups for an individual, household or relationship line; narrative reports in continuous readable prose; particularities and person analysis highlighting unusual details or patterns.
2. Plausibility and Data Quality Reports: date and relationship consistency checks before publication; a source list compiling citations and distinguishing evidence from unsubstantiated claims.
3. Lists and Reference Reports: descendancy and marriage lists; places, events and anniversaries; Ahnentafel and genogram; timeline and name distribution; sociogram and influential people analysis.
4. Chart and Diagram Reports: fan, hourglass and relationship charts; Ahnentafel and genogram diagrams; timeline and name distribution charts.
5. Reports include the person's profile picture. The editable report behaves like a filled-in form, retaining the page design while values are edited.
6. Person and Kinship reports explain named first, second, third and later cousin relationships, including once, twice, thrice and later removals, and distinguish cousin-in-law connections.
7. Latest layout clarification: below the portrait/title header, section bands and body details return to the same left edge, without extra indentation. Latest type-size clarification: default report text is 15 pt, including header facts and explanations; section headings stay bold. This intentionally enlarges the compact type seen in the MacFamilyTree reference.
8. Corresponding MacFamilyTree 11 report content/features must be implemented, not just their titles. See KinForge-Report-Content-Parity.md for the manual-based comparison and explicit unfinished work.
9. Every included glyph must have its meaning labelled beneath it, or explained in a report key. This applies to person cards, family trees, report charts, kinship reports and exports. Private annotations remain opt-in.

The user-supplied GitHub destination returned 404 during this task. The supplied list remains the requirement. Synium's official Visualize & Explore page and the MacFamilyTree 11.1 English manual were used as product references, not copied product code or runtime dependencies.

## Boundaries

- These are built-in calculations over the user's recorded data. No provider is contacted to generate a report.
- Ahnentafel cannot assign conventional paternal/maternal slot numbers when parent roles are unspecified. It lists those links instead of guessing from gender.
- Fan images show at most five ancestor generations for readability; the other generation-limited reports allow up to ten. Large static diagrams are accompanied by their textual data.
- Genogram symbols use recorded gender and relationship status, not inferred biological sex or medical conditions. Sociograms include only the relationship types currently stored by KinForge, not unrecorded friendships.
- Influential people analysis is explicitly connection centrality, not a claim of historical influence. Missing evidence is not automatically speculation or falsehood.
- The formatted draft is a continuous editing surface, paginated for export/printing, not MacFamilyTree's full free-positioned layout engine. Embedded charts are regenerated snapshots, not individually draggable objects inside the document. A fillable PDF form with AcroForm fields is not implemented; the in-app draft fields are editable.
- RTF preserves basic text formatting and embedded images but reflows header columns. Text/CSV cannot contain photographs. External or SVG image URLs are not inserted into reports.
- Downloaded PDFs use high-resolution rendered pages to preserve the editable report's exact visual layout and fonts. They are image-based, not searchable text or AcroForm documents. Browser Print can create a text-based PDF; Text and RTF remain available for text reuse. Export renders one page at a time to avoid oversized full-document canvases.
- Existing other report menu entries outside this requested catalogue retain their prior limitations. This change does not certify every feature in Ideas.md or every report option in MacFamilyTree.
- Release installers were not rebuilt or revalidated in this report work. Local preview/source verification is distinct from deployment and native package delivery.
