# KinForge Glyph System

Created 23 September 2026. This delivers a local glyph library and its integration into individual profiles. It does not complete the full Ideas.md application requirements or constitute a new published/native release.

## Design

- 67 glyph definitions: 8 relationship events, 17 other life-event/fallback glyphs, 17 disability categories, 22 access-needs categories and 3 record-status glyphs.
- 24-unit view boxes, 1.65-unit rounded strokes, unfilled line art and inherited text color. The preview supports 16-40 px sizes and light/dark appearances.
- Inspired by the compact, labelled pictograms observed in MacFamilyTree. KinForge's mappings and presentation are its own; the actual vector artwork uses the existing Lucide library under its included licence. No MacFamilyTree artwork was extracted or copied.
- Different concepts have explicit names. Color is not required to understand a symbol. These are KinForge conventions, not standardized medical or legal notation.

## Working Integration

- **Person > Access needs:** add, edit and remove multiple private annotations. The picker separates Disabilities from Access needs, and supports searches such as CP, blind, deaf and amputee. Record labels and details are editable, with an optional source.
- **Person > Overview:** a labelled, private summary, grouped separately into Disabilities and Access needs. No person is automatically assigned any disability or support category.
- **Person > Timeline and Edit details:** built-in life events have matching glyphs, including distinct separation, divorce and annulment icons. Custom/unrecognized events retain their own text label and use a neutral event fallback.
- **Symbol legend:** searchable names and meanings in a modal with keyboard-operable controls.
- **Save behavior:** annotations use the existing local persistence and undo history. Older records without the new optional field remain valid. Duplicate-person merging preserves annotations.

These glyphs do not automatically change family relationships, chart connectors, diagnoses, legal status or application accessibility settings. Private needs are not placed on public-facing tree nodes. Full chart/printed-report notation is a separate, unfinished integration.

## Privacy Boundary

New annotations are always private. They are omitted from the existing website, GEDCOM, CSV, report and list output paths and hidden by print styling. Full JSON backups retain them so they can be restored.

The current application stores its workspace locally. The private flag is not encryption or a new access-control system. Anyone with access to that browser profile, workspace or a full backup may be able to read the data. The work did not publish or upload any person's health/access information.
